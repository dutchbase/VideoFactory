# Audience and video types

Viewers and the default language come from the selected brand's `brands/<id>/brand.md` and this video's brief. Do not assume one brand's audience fits another. One video has one language. A translation is a new video, for example `videos/<brand>/<slug>-<lang>/`.

| Type | Goal | Format | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| course | teach one skill in a lesson | wide 16:9 | 2–8 min |
| short | hook + one idea for TikTok, Reels or Shorts | tall 9:16 | 15–60 s |
| ad | one benefit + call to action | tall, wide cut optional | 15–30 s |
| explainer | explain one mechanism or decision | wide or tall, per brief | enough to deliver the promised explanation |
| founder story | show an actual decision and its consequences | wide or tall, per brief | enough for the real story |
| onboarding | help a new customer do one step | wide | 30 s–2 min |

Write the type, language and call to action in `01_voice/brief.md`.
