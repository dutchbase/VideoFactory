# library: shelf of reusable assets
Reference material for every video. What it holds, by category and kind: `CATALOG.md` (generated, read it first). Product (per-video copies) lives in `videos/<brand>/<slug>/04_scene/assets/`.

**Reads:** `sources.json` (allowed sources and licences), `categories.json` (the one list of categories), `upstream/sources.json` (component sites, pinned commits). Guide: `_shared/library.md`.
**Does:** `python anim.py lib ...` adds icons, Lottie and SVG art here. `python anim.py stock ...` downloads stock to `cache/` (git-ignored). `python anim.py use <slug> <ref>` copies one item into a video and logs it in `assets/library.md`.
**Writes:** `icons/` (static), `icons-animated/` (animated icons), `lottie/`, `graphics/`, `blocks/` (HyperFrames scene blocks, `blocks/CONTEXT.md`) and `fx/` (parts for both engines, `fx/CONTEXT.md`), all committed. `upstream/<source>.json`: every component of the four sites we can rewrite (generated by `lib upstream`). `cache/` (ignored; upstream clones are data, never run). `INDEX.json` and `CATALOG.md` (generated by `lib index`, never hand-edited). Brand-owned assets, such as a motion pack, live in `brands/<brand>/library/`. `audio/` is the sound library: local and git-ignored, except its contract and source list (`audio/CONTEXT.md`).
**Human check:** look at the licence line that `use` prints. Look at the contact sheet from `lib gallery`.

Allowed licences: CC0, MIT, ISC, Apache-2.0, Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash, Lottie Simple; for sound only also Sonniss GDC and Mixkit Free. Nothing that needs attribution.
