# Narration draft

Replace the first fenced block with narration. Write for the ear: _shared/voice.md. Wording rules: _shared/tone.md.
```
An explainer video usually means three tools, a timeline and a lost weekend.

VideoFactory turns all of that into one conversation.

It is one folder on your computer. Your AI agent, Claude Code or Codex, does the work. You make the choices, and you check every step.

First, the setup. You need Linux, Python three point twelve, FFmpeg, and Claude Code or Codex. Type slash V F setup. The agent installs the rest, checks your computer, and renders a short test video.

Everything else is optional. Whisper gives every word a time. ElevenLabs gives you a paid AI voice. Free keys from Pexels, Pixabay and Unsplash unlock stock video and photos. Earshot lets the agent listen to the sound. Node adds an advanced animation engine. And Brainstormform shows the agent's questions as a form in your browser.

Not sure what to do next? Type slash V F. It reads your folders and tells you the next step. Slash V F help lists every command.

Every video starts with a brand. Slash V F new brand asks for your colours, fonts, logo and voice. Slash V F new style designs a new look. Slash V F new character creates a mascot, with AI images that you approve batch by batch.

Then type slash V F new video. Pick your brand, wide or tall, and write a short brief: who the video is for, and what they must do after.

Need facts? Slash V F research collects them from public web pages, with a source for each one.

Slash V F script writes the narration with you. A tone check removes hype and empty words before anything is recorded.

Slash V F voice makes the voice. Record yourself for free, or let ElevenLabs read the script. A paid voice only runs after you approve the script in the Studio.

Slash V F transcript gives every word an exact time.

Slash V F cues marks the moments when the picture must change.

Now the fun part. Slash V F scene builds the animation. It takes icons, graphics and Lottie animations from the library. It finds stock video, photos and music. It takes screenshots and screen recordings of real websites. It drops in ready-made motion components. And it adds sound effects and music, from free CC0 files, or generated on your own computer.

Before you see anything, the agent takes a still at every cue. A checker counts the problems, and the agent fixes them until every counter reads zero.

Slash V F render makes the final video file. Voice, music and effects are mixed and leveled. You also get a phone copy, a thumbnail, and chapters for YouTube. With Earshot, the agent even listens to the result.

The Studio is a web page on your own computer. It shows every video, and the pages where you review and approve.

Slash V F status shows where each video is.

Each command does one step, and then it stops. Nothing is paid or published without a clear yes from you.

This video was made with those same commands.

VideoFactory is free and open source. Clone the repo, type slash V F setup, and make your first video today.
```
