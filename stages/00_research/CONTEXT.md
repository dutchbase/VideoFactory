# 00 Research (optional)

Use this stage when you want facts from public websites for a video. Skip it when you supply all facts. If you skip it, record the reason in `REVIEW.md`.

Start with `python anim.py research <slug>`. Input: your topic and question, plus Audience and One outcome in `01_voice/brief.md`.

Process: search, pick up to 10 pages, read each page, save it in `pages/`, fill `sources.md`, write `research.md` (Findings, Conflicts, Open points). Follow `_shared/research.md`.

Output: `research.md` and `sources.md`. You check each claim against its source, remove weak sources and decide conflicts. Record the decision in `REVIEW.md`. Then write `brief.md` and `script.md` from the approved notes only.

Handoff: mark the current `research` gate ready and open its exact Studio review link (`_shared/dashboard-review.md`). Guided command: `/vf-research`. Next: `/vf-script`.
