# Character shelf

Characters come from the character workshop (`_shared/character-workshop.md`, guided: `/vf-new-character`, skeleton: `_templates/character-factory/`). No character records ship with VideoFactory. A folder `characters/<id>/` appears when you make one.

Each workshop character keeps one editable `character.md` profile, three verified concepts in `01_design/candidates.json`, and one approved canonical `02_identity/reference-set.json`. A variant in `03_animation/<style>/` keeps that identity and gets its own per-action review.
