## Look
Tells what is new, why it matters, and how to get it. A dark tease, a big reveal of the name, then new items with tags.

## Behaviour
- Structure (percent of runtime): 1) tease: one line, 0–8%, 2) reveal: the name and the hero visual, 8–22%, 3) what is new: 2–3 changes, 22–60%, 4) why it matters: the benefit for the viewer, 60–78%, 5) proof or numbers, 78–88%, 6) how to get it, date and price, 88–100%.
- Tease: dark, almost empty frame, one line of text (`P.ease.softLand`, 400 ms). Reveal: one hard cut or a scale from 0.92 to 1 of the hero (`P.ease.enter`, 700 ms) with the name in large type.
- Each new item: a "New" tag pops (`P.spring` pop) with its name; hold 4–6 s. Old behaviour appears small and dim beside it (35%) only if the contrast helps.
- Date and where to get it: large text, hold 3 s, button pill pops.
- Changes per minute: 8–12. Wide 30–90 s, tall 15–45 s. Sound: a rise into the reveal, one hit on the name.

## Do
- Say the name early and often.
- Give a date or a place to get it.
- Use one hero visual.

## Don't
- No feature dump; max 3 items.
- No claims you cannot show.
- No call to action without a place or date.

## Build notes
Stage 03 cues: tease line, reveal cut, tag + item per change, date, button. The hero is a large Modern kit component or screenshot.
