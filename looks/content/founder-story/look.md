## Look
A personal story of one real decision and what followed, told as a clear arc. Calm scenes with large type, dates and real photos.

## Behaviour
- Structure (percent of runtime): 1) hook: the moment of the decision or the cost, 0–8%, 2) before: life or work as it was, 8–25%, 3) the decision, said plainly, with the reason, 25–45%, 4) consequences: what went wrong and right, with real numbers or dates, 45–80%, 5) what was learned, 80–93%, 6) invitation or call to action, 93–100%.
- Pace: slower than other formats; a change every 5–9 s. Use hold time: the decision line holds 3 s on screen as large text (`P.ease.softLand`, 400 ms).
- Visuals: one real photo or clip when available (slow push 1.0 to 1.06 over the shot, `P.ease.linear`); otherwise one large quote or number per scene. Date stamps in mono text mark the time jumps.
- Transitions between time periods: cross dissolve 800 ms or a slow slide (`P.ease.move`, 900 ms).
- The hardest moment gets a pause: 1.5 s of still frame, no motion, no music change.
- Wide 2–6 min; tall 45–90 s. Changes per minute: 5–8. Sound: warm bed under the voice, silence at the hard moment.

## Do
- Tell only true facts and real numbers.
- Keep the decision in one plain sentence.
- Show a cost, not only a success.

## Don't
- No invented dialogue.
- No hero tone; say the doubt.
- Do not skip the consequences.

## Build notes
Stage 03 cues: date stamp, decision line, number, quote. Photos go in the brand library or `_shared/capture.md`. Needs a person's approval for any personal claim.
