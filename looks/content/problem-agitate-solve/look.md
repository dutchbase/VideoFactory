## Look
Ad structure: name the pain, make its cost felt, then offer the solution and a call to action. Cold pain scenes, a hard turn, then bright solution scenes.

## Behaviour
- Structure (percent of runtime): 1) hook with the pain in the viewer's words, 0–10% (first 1–2 s must show the pain), 2) problem 10–25%, 3) agitate: cost in time, money or feeling, 25–50%, 4) solve: product and the one benefit 50–85%, 5) proof line and call to action 85–100%. The source guides suggest about 15% problem, 20% agitate, 65% solve.
- Pain section: tight, cold look, dim tones, fast cuts every 2–3 s, text slams in (`P.ease.crashIn`, 330 ms). Use danger colour for the cost number.
- Turn point: one hard cut or whip on the word "until" or "now" (`P.ease.whip`, 230 ms) into the bright state with the accent colour.
- Solve section: slower, a change every 3–4 s (`P.ease.softLand`, 400 ms). Show the product doing the one job.
- Call to action: pill button pops (`P.spring` pop) and holds 2–3 s with the URL or action in large text.
- Length: tall 15–30 s; wide cut 15–30 s. Changes per minute: 20–30 in pain, 12–18 in solve. Sound: tense low tone in the pain, release and rise at the turn.

## Do
- Open on the pain, not on the logo.
- Show one cost number.
- Name one benefit, one call to action.

## Don't
- No more than one product claim.
- No pain section longer than 45% of the runtime.
- No call to action hidden under captions.

## Build notes
Stage 03 cues: pain words, cost number, turn cut, benefit, call to action. Use two look moods in one video: cold before the turn, bright after.
