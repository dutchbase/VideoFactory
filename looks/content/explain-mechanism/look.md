## Look
A single system diagram that grows: parts appear one at a time and are joined by arrows. The finished diagram is the summary frame.

## Behaviour
- Structure (the voice drives it): 1) hook question or surprising effect, 2) the parts, named one by one, 3) the chain of cause and effect, step by step, 4) the "so what" for the viewer, 5) one-line recap on the full diagram.
- Each part enters on the word that names it; each arrow draws on the verb that links two parts ("sends", "turns into").
- Zoom into one part for a detail, then pull back to the whole diagram so the viewer keeps the map.
- Keep earlier parts on screen (dimmed to 50%) while new ones arrive; never rebuild the diagram from zero.
- Length: 60–180 s wide, 30–60 s tall. One mechanism per video.

## Do
- Name every part with one or two words.
- Use the same position for the same part all video long.
- End on the complete diagram plus the takeaway sentence.

## Don't
- No more than seven parts in one diagram; split into two scenes.
- No history or side stories in the middle of the chain.
- No step that the voice does not say.

## Build notes
Stage 03 cues: one cue per part and one per arrow. Arrows: SVG paths with dash reveals. The camera pull-back is a scale on the scene root (`_shared/motion.md`, Camera).
