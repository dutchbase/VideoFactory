## Look
Short-form: a hook in 1-2 seconds, one idea, a payoff, and an ending that loops. One huge hook line, big captions and one payoff, in tall frame.

## Behaviour
- Structure (seconds, for a 30 s video; scale for 15–60 s): 1) hook 0–2 s: the claim, the question or the result, first word on frame 1, 2) setup 2–6 s: why it matters in one line, 3) the one idea 6–22 s: 2–3 beats, 4) payoff 22–27 s: the answer or result, 5) loop 27–30 s: the last line leads back to the first frame.
- Frame 1 already has the hook text, large (about 25% of frame height). No logo intro, no fade from black.
- Pace: a visible change every 1.5–3 s. Text enters on the word (`P.ease.crashIn`, 330 ms for the hook; `P.ease.enter`, 300 ms for others). Cuts use `P.ease.whip` (230 ms).
- Word captions: 2–4 words at once, 0.3–0.4 s per word. Key word in accent.
- Payoff: scale pop 1.0 to 1.08 (`P.spring` pop) and hold 1.5 s. Loop: the final frame matches the start frame so the replay is seamless; a cut or the same text in a new state works.
- Changes per minute: 25–40. Length: 15–60 s, best 20–35 s. Safe zone: keep text out of the bottom 20% and top 12%. Sound: beat or rhythm bed, hit on the hook, hit on the payoff.

## Do
- Put the strongest line in the first 2 s.
- Make one idea, one claim, one payoff.
- Make the last frame fit the first.

## Don't
- No intro, no greeting.
- No second idea.
- No hold longer than 3 s without a change.
