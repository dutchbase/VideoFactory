## Look
A case study: the state before, what changed, the state after, with numbers. Two states in one fixed frame, joined by a wipe.

## Behaviour
- Structure (percent of runtime): 1) hook: the result number 0–8%, 2) who and what: the client or case in one line 8–15%, 3) BEFORE state with one number 15–35%, 4) the change: 2–3 actions 35–60%, 5) AFTER state with the same number measured again 60–85%, 6) lesson and call to action 85–100%.
- Before and after use the same frame, same layout, same metric position. Only the values change.
- Before: dim, desaturated card, number in the danger colour (`P.ease.enter`, 450 ms). The change: a wipe or slider line moves across the frame (`P.ease.move`, 800 ms), steps listed in order as chips.
- After: bright accent state; the number counts from the before value to the after value (1000 ms, `P.ease.softLand`), and the difference chip ("+240%") pops (`P.spring` pop).
- Hold the before 4–6 s, the after 5–7 s. Changes per minute: 6–10. Wide 45–120 s, tall 20–45 s.
- Sound: flat tone in the before, a rise in the change, a clean hit at the after number.

## Do
- Measure the same thing before and after.
- Show the time span and the source of the numbers.
- Keep the frame identical.

## Don't
- No result without the baseline.
- No invented numbers; use real data only.
- No more than three actions in the change.

## Build notes
Stage 03 cues: before in, before number, change steps, wipe, after number, delta chip. The wipe is a clip-path on the after layer.
